## **CORE VALUES**
*(From Uganda National Ethics and Values Policy 2013)*

### **Foundational Values Underpinning Education:**
1. **RESPECT FOR HUMANITY AND ENVIRONMENT**
   - Core principle for all interactions and sustainability

2. **HONESTY**
   - Uphold and defend the truth at all times
   - Foundation for academic and professional integrity

3. **JUSTICE AND FAIRNESS**
   - In dealing with others
   - Essential for equitable learning environments

4. **HARD WORK**
   - For self-reliance
   - Basis for achievement and perseverance

5. **INTEGRITY**
   - Moral uprightness and sound character
   - Professional and personal ethics foundation

6. **CREATIVITY AND INNOVATIVENESS**
   - Driving forces for progress and problem-solving

7. **SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY**
   - Accountability to community and society

8. **SOCIAL HARMONY**
   - Peaceful coexistence and collaboration

9. **NATIONAL UNITY**
   - Collective identity and purpose

10. **NATIONAL CONSCIOUSNESS AND PATRIOTISM**
    - Pride and contribution to national development

*Note: These values inform all teaching and learning but are not directly assessed.*

## **KEY LEARNING OUTCOMES**

### **Four Pillars of Graduate Profile:**

#### **1. SELF-ASSURED INDIVIDUALS**
*Graduates who:*
- Demonstrate self-motivation, self-management, and self-esteem
- Know their own preferences, strengths, and limitations
- Adjust behavior and language appropriately to different social situations
- Relate well to a range of personality types

#### **2. RESPONSIBLE AND PATRIOTIC CITIZENS**
*Graduates who:*
- Cherish the values promoted in the curriculum
- Promote equity, indigenous cultures, and languages
- Appreciate other people's cultures
- Apply environmental and health awareness in decision-making
- Have positive identity as individuals and global citizens
- Are motivated to contribute to personal, community, and national well-being

#### **3. LIFELONG LEARNERS**
*Graduates who:*
- Can plan, reflect, and direct their own learning
- Actively seek lifelong learning opportunities for personal/professional development

#### **4. POSITIVE CONTRIBUTORS TO SOCIETY**
*Graduates who:*
- Have acquired and can apply Generic Skills
- Demonstrate understanding of emerging societal and economic needs
- Understand how to design, make, and critically evaluate products/processes
- Appreciate the physical, biological, and technological world
- Make informed decisions about sustainable development and its impact

## **GENERIC SKILLS (21st Century Competencies)**
*Embedded across all subjects and disciplines*

### **1. CRITICAL THINKING AND PROBLEM-SOLVING**
- Plan and carry out investigations
- Sort and analyze information
- Identify problems and ways forward
- Predict outcomes and make reasoned decisions
- Evaluate different solutions

### **2. CO-OPERATION AND SELF-DIRECTED LEARNING**
- Work effectively in diverse teams
- Interact effectively with others
- Take responsibility for own learning
- Work independently with persistence
- Manage goals and time

### **3. CREATIVITY AND INNOVATION**
- Use imagination to explore possibilities
- Work with others to generate ideas
- Suggest and develop new solutions
- Try innovative alternatives
- Look for patterns and make generalizations

### **4. MATHEMATICAL COMPUTATION AND ICT PROFICIENCY**
- Use numbers and measurements accurately
- Interpret and interrogate mathematical data
- Use mathematics to justify and support decisions
- Use technology to create, manipulate, and process information
- Use technology to collaborate, communicate, and refine work

### **5. COMMUNICATION**
- Listen attentively and with comprehension
- Talk confidently and explain ideas/opinions clearly
- Read accurately and fluently
- Write and present coherently
- Use a range of media to communicate ideas

### **Assessment Principles for Higher Education:**
- Focus on **understanding** over memorization
- Application of knowledge in **varied contexts**
- Demonstration of **transferable skills**
- **Diverse assessment methods**: oral, written, performance, practical
- Integration of **self-assessment and reflection**
- Balance of **formative and summative evaluation**
